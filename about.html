About me

I'm an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from Jashore University of Science and Technology, Bangladesh, with a particular interest in building intelligent systems that connect software with the physical world.

My interests sit across embedded systems, artificial intelligence, biomedical engineering, EVs and energy optimization. My research has led me toward sEMG-based prosthetic control and edge AI, while my engineering projects have taken me into computer vision, control systems, energy recovery and industrial energy efficiency.

I also enjoy teaching and mentoring. Outside engineering, I'm interested in cinema, books, cricket, travel, photography, cycling and writing.
