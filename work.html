Research

Development and Able-Bodied Validation of a Resource-Efficient Three-Sensor sEMG Prosthetic Arm Prototype Using Edge-Deployed Deep Learning

My undergraduate thesis: a low-cost, 3D-printed trans-radial prosthetic arm controlled by three surface EMG channels. A 1D-CNN classifies six hand gestures and runs directly on an ESP32-S3.

About 18.5 ms inference and control latency on ESP32-S3Accuracy and F1 results: [PLACEHOLDER, add once you share the numbers]

Focus

  • sEMG-based gesture recognition
  • Minimal sensor configuration
  • 1D-CNN and edge deployment
  • ESP32-S3 and prosthetic control

Method

GRABMyo-based channel analysis and sensor selection, sEMG acquisition, preprocessing, gesture classification, model training, embedded deployment and real-time validation.

Hardware

Three EMG V3 sensors, ESP32-S3, PCA9685, MG996R and SG90 servos, a 3S 11.1 V LiPo battery and a 3D-printed hand.

Projects

AI, computer vision, intelligent transportation

LimitEye

A third-year project. A custom YOLOv8 model, annotated with LabelImg and trained on Google Colab, detects vehicles and license plates. SORT tracking gives each vehicle an ID, speed comes from pixel-to-distance conversion, and a violation triggers an automated e-fine by email.

  • YOLOv8
  • SORT
  • Python
  • Google Colab

EV, energy recovery, embedded systems

Regenerative braking for electric wheelchairs

A concept that returns braking energy to a 24 V battery pack, with ultrasonic and MPU6050 safety sensing. A Python model estimates 5 to 10% direct energy recovery. Presented as a poster at a symposium on energy security and fuel optimization.

  • Arduino/ESP32
  • MPU6050
  • Python
Energy optimization, industrial engineering

Industrial energy audit

An on-site audit of Asian Marble Industries, a 118,603 sq ft facility using 22,771 kWh a month. Power factor improvement and LED retrofitting were projected to cut electricity costs by 10 to 15%.
